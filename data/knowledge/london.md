# London, United Kingdom — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** London  
**Country:** United Kingdom  
**Themes:** museums, history, theatre, food, shopping

## Attractions

The British Museum, Tower of London, Westminster and Big Ben, the London Eye, and free national galleries.

## Food

Pub roasts, fish and chips, Borough Market, global street food, and a strong Indian curry scene.

## Culture

West End theatre, royal history, world-class (often free) museums, and diverse neighbourhoods.

## Transportation

The Underground and buses run on contactless tap-in; an Oyster or contactless card caps daily fares.

## Safety

Safe overall; mind pickpockets in tourist crowds and look right when crossing streets.

## Accommodation

Mid-range hotels in Kensington and Bloomsbury; budget chains and boutique stays across zones.

## Activities

A West End show, a Thames walk, day trips to Oxford or Windsor, and market browsing.

## Weather

October is cool and often wet (8-15C); an umbrella and layers are essential.

## Local Customs

Queue politely, stand on the right of escalators, and tipping is around 10-12.5% when not included.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for London: mid-range hotels around $161 per night, daily food budget about $40 per person, and local transport roughly $14 per person per day. Paid attractions average $29 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in London means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
