# Singapore, Singapore — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Singapore  
**Country:** Singapore  
**Themes:** food, family, shopping, nature, culture

## Attractions

Gardens by the Bay, Marina Bay Sands, Sentosa, the Merlion, and Chinatown and Little India.

## Food

Hawker centres for Hainanese chicken rice, laksa, chilli crab, satay, and kaya toast.

## Culture

A multicultural blend of Chinese, Malay, Indian, and Peranakan heritage.

## Transportation

The clean, cheap MRT covers the island; an EZ-Link card makes it seamless.

## Safety

One of the safest cities globally; note strict laws on littering and jaywalking.

## Accommodation

Luxury towers at Marina Bay, mid-range hotels in Chinatown, and boutique shophouse stays.

## Activities

A Gardens by the Bay light show, a Sentosa beach day, and a hawker-centre food tour.

## Weather

Hot and humid year-round with afternoon showers; light clothing and an umbrella help.

## Local Customs

No chewing gum sales, no eating on the MRT, and tipping is not customary.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Singapore: mid-range hotels around $154 per night, daily food budget about $38 per person, and local transport roughly $13 per person per day. Paid attractions average $28 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Singapore means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
