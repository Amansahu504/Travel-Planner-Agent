# New York, United States — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** New York  
**Country:** United States  
**Themes:** museums, food, shopping, nightlife, art

## Attractions

Central Park, the Statue of Liberty, the Met and MoMA, Times Square, and the High Line.

## Food

Bagels, pizza slices, deli sandwiches, global cuisine in every borough, and food halls.

## Culture

Broadway theatre, jazz clubs, world-class museums, and endlessly diverse neighbourhoods.

## Transportation

The 24/7 subway is the fastest way around; pay by contactless OMNY tap.

## Safety

Busy and generally safe in tourist areas; keep standard big-city awareness at night.

## Accommodation

Midtown mid-range hotels, boutique stays in SoHo, and budget options across the boroughs.

## Activities

A Broadway show, a Central Park stroll, a ferry past the Statue of Liberty, and museum days.

## Weather

October is crisp and pleasant (10-18C) with autumn colour in the parks.

## Local Customs

Tipping 18-20% is expected at restaurants; walk briskly and stand right on escalators.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for New York: mid-range hotels around $168 per night, daily food budget about $42 per person, and local transport roughly $14 per person per day. Paid attractions average $30 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in New York means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
