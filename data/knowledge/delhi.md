# Delhi, India — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Delhi  
**Country:** India  
**Themes:** history, food, culture, shopping

## Attractions

The Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, India Gate, and the markets of Old Delhi.

## Food

Chaat, butter chicken, parathas, kebabs, and street food in Chandni Chowk.

## Culture

A layering of Mughal and colonial history alongside modern Indian life.

## Transportation

The clean, efficient Delhi Metro is the best way around; use app-based cabs otherwise.

## Safety

Take care with traffic and touts; women travellers should prefer registered transport at night.

## Accommodation

Heritage havelis, mid-range hotels in South Delhi, and budget stays in Paharganj.

## Activities

A Old Delhi food and rickshaw tour, monument visits, and a day trip to the Taj Mahal in Agra.

## Weather

October is pleasant post-monsoon (18-32C); winter mornings can be foggy.

## Local Customs

Remove shoes at temples, dress modestly, and bargaining is expected in markets.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Delhi: mid-range hotels around $70 per night, daily food budget about $18 per person, and local transport roughly $6 per person per day. Paid attractions average $12 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Delhi means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
