# San Francisco, United States — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** San Francisco  
**Country:** United States  
**Themes:** nature, technology, food, culture

## Attractions

The Golden Gate Bridge, Alcatraz, Fisherman's Wharf, and cable cars over steep hills.

## Food

Sourdough bread bowls, Mission burritos, Dungeness crab, and a strong farm-to-table scene.

## Culture

Tech innovation, counterculture history in Haight-Ashbury, and vibrant Chinatown and Mission murals.

## Transportation

Cable cars, Muni, and BART; a Clipper card covers regional transit including to the airport.

## Safety

Take care with valuables; avoid leaving anything visible in parked cars.

## Accommodation

Union Square hotels, boutique stays near North Beach, and budget options in the outer districts.

## Activities

Biking the Golden Gate Bridge, an Alcatraz tour, and a day trip to Muir Woods or wine country.

## Weather

Mild but foggy; October is often the sunniest, warmest month, though evenings are cool.

## Local Customs

Tipping 18-20% is standard; layers are essential for the microclimates.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for San Francisco: mid-range hotels around $168 per night, daily food budget about $42 per person, and local transport roughly $14 per person per day. Paid attractions average $30 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in San Francisco means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
