# Sydney, Australia — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Sydney  
**Country:** Australia  
**Themes:** beach, nature, food, family

## Attractions

The Opera House, Harbour Bridge, Bondi Beach, the Royal Botanic Garden, and Darling Harbour.

## Food

Fresh seafood, brunch culture, multicultural eats, and the Sydney Fish Market.

## Culture

A relaxed outdoor lifestyle, harbour culture, and Aboriginal heritage sites.

## Transportation

Trains, ferries, and buses on a tap-and-go Opal card; ferries double as scenic rides.

## Safety

Very safe; swim between the flags and respect surf and sun conditions.

## Accommodation

Harbour-view hotels at The Rocks, mid-range in the CBD, and beachside stays at Bondi.

## Activities

The Bondi-to-Coogee coastal walk, an Opera House tour, a harbour ferry, and Blue Mountains day trips.

## Weather

October is spring: mild and pleasant (14-22C), warming toward summer.

## Local Customs

Casual and friendly; tipping is not expected but appreciated for great service.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Sydney: mid-range hotels around $154 per night, daily food budget about $38 per person, and local transport roughly $13 per person per day. Paid attractions average $28 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Sydney means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
