# Edinburgh, United Kingdom — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Edinburgh  
**Country:** United Kingdom  
**Themes:** history, culture, nature, festivals

## Attractions

Edinburgh Castle, the Royal Mile, Arthur's Seat, and the Palace of Holyroodhouse.

## Food

Haggis, Scottish salmon, shortbread, and a growing whisky and gin bar scene.

## Culture

Literary heritage, the world-famous Fringe festival in August, and Gothic old-town charm.

## Transportation

A compact, walkable centre with trams and buses; the airport tram is convenient.

## Safety

Very safe; prepare for changeable weather when hiking Arthur's Seat.

## Accommodation

Historic hotels near the Royal Mile and cosy guesthouses in the New Town.

## Activities

A castle visit, a hike up Arthur's Seat, a whisky tasting, and ghost tours.

## Weather

Cool and windy; October is crisp with early sunsets, so pack warm layers.

## Local Customs

Friendly and informal; tipping around 10% is appreciated but not mandatory.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Edinburgh: mid-range hotels around $161 per night, daily food budget about $40 per person, and local transport roughly $14 per person per day. Paid attractions average $29 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Edinburgh means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
