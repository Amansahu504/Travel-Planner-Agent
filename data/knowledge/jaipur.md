# Jaipur, India — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Jaipur  
**Country:** India  
**Themes:** history, culture, shopping, architecture

## Attractions

Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, and Nahargarh Fort views.

## Food

Dal baati churma, laal maas, ghewar sweets, and Rajasthani thali feasts.

## Culture

The 'Pink City' of Rajasthan, rich in royal heritage, crafts, and folk music.

## Transportation

Autos and app cabs are easiest; the compact old city is walkable.

## Safety

Generally safe for tourists; agree auto fares in advance and beware gem scams.

## Accommodation

Palace hotels, heritage havelis, and budget guesthouses near the old city.

## Activities

A fort and palace circuit, a block-printing workshop, and bazaar shopping for textiles.

## Weather

October is warm and dry, a comfortable time to visit after the monsoon.

## Local Customs

Dress modestly, remove shoes at temples, and haggle politely in the bazaars.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Jaipur: mid-range hotels around $70 per night, daily food budget about $18 per person, and local transport roughly $6 per person per day. Paid attractions average $12 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Jaipur means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
