# Bangkok, Thailand — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Bangkok  
**Country:** Thailand  
**Themes:** food, temples, nightlife, shopping, culture

## Attractions

The Grand Palace and Wat Pho, Wat Arun, floating markets, and Chatuchak weekend market.

## Food

Pad thai, som tam, mango sticky rice, boat noodles, and street food everywhere.

## Culture

Ornate Buddhist temples, tuk-tuk chaos, and a warm 'Land of Smiles' hospitality.

## Transportation

The BTS Skytrain and MRT beat the traffic; river boats ply the Chao Phraya.

## Safety

Generally safe; use metered taxis or apps and be wary of gem-shop scams.

## Accommodation

Affordable luxury riverside hotels, mid-range stays near the BTS, and Khao San budget rooms.

## Activities

A temple circuit, a long-tail boat canal tour, a cooking class, and rooftop-bar evenings.

## Weather

Hot year-round; October is the tail of the rainy season with short heavy showers.

## Local Customs

Dress modestly at temples, never touch someone's head, and show respect for the monarchy.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Bangkok: mid-range hotels around $77 per night, daily food budget about $19 per person, and local transport roughly $7 per person per day. Paid attractions average $14 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Bangkok means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
