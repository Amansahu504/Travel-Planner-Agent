# Madrid, Spain — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Madrid  
**Country:** Spain  
**Themes:** art, food, nightlife, culture

## Attractions

The Prado, Reina Sofia, Retiro Park, the Royal Palace, and Plaza Mayor.

## Food

Cocido stew, bocadillo de calamares, churros con chocolate, and a legendary tapas scene.

## Culture

The Golden Triangle of art museums, flamenco, and famously late nightlife.

## Transportation

A clean, extensive metro; the centre is very walkable.

## Safety

Safe overall; keep an eye on bags in busy plazas and on the metro.

## Accommodation

Central hotels near Sol and Gran Via; budget options in Lavapies.

## Activities

An art-museum day, a flamenco show, a Retiro Park picnic, and tapas hopping in La Latina.

## Weather

October is pleasant and dry (10-20C) with clear skies.

## Local Customs

Long lunches and late dinners; a siesta lull closes some shops mid-afternoon.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Madrid: mid-range hotels around $126 per night, daily food budget about $32 per person, and local transport roughly $11 per person per day. Paid attractions average $22 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Madrid means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
