# Melbourne, Australia — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Melbourne  
**Country:** Australia  
**Themes:** food, art, culture, sport

## Attractions

Federation Square, laneway street art, the Queen Victoria Market, and the MCG.

## Food

A world-class coffee scene, laneway cafes, diverse dining, and rooftop bars.

## Culture

Australia's cultural capital: galleries, live music, sport, and festivals.

## Transportation

Free tram zone in the CBD; a Myki card covers trams, trains, and buses.

## Safety

Safe and walkable; standard care at night in nightlife districts.

## Accommodation

Design hotels in the CBD, boutique stays in Fitzroy, and budget options near Southbank.

## Activities

A laneway coffee crawl, a Great Ocean Road day trip, and a sports or arts event.

## Weather

Famously changeable ('four seasons in a day'); spring October needs layers.

## Local Customs

Coffee culture is serious; casual dress is the norm.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Melbourne: mid-range hotels around $154 per night, daily food budget about $38 per person, and local transport roughly $13 per person per day. Paid attractions average $28 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Melbourne means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
