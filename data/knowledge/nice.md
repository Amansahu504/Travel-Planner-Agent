**Destination:** Nice  
**Country:** France  
**Themes:** beach, food, relaxation, art

## Attractions

The Promenade des Anglais, Old Town (Vieux Nice), Castle Hill viewpoint, and the Matisse Museum.

## Food

Socca chickpea pancakes, salade nicoise, ratatouille, and fresh Mediterranean seafood.

## Culture

French Riviera art scene with Matisse and Chagall, plus a relaxed seaside pace.

## Transportation

A walkable centre with trams; regional trains reach Monaco, Cannes, and Antibes in minutes.

## Safety

Generally safe; typical beach-town care with valuables on the pebbled shore.

## Accommodation

Seafront hotels along the Promenade, and affordable stays in the Old Town.

## Activities

Beach days, coastal train hops to Monaco, and sunset drinks overlooking the bay.

## Weather

Mild Mediterranean climate; October remains pleasant for coastal walks.

## Local Customs

A relaxed Riviera etiquette; a friendly 'Bonjour' still goes a long way.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Nice: mid-range hotels around $147 per night, daily food budget about $37 per person, and local transport roughly $13 per person per day. Paid attractions average $26 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Nice means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
