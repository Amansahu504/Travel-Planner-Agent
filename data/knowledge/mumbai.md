# Mumbai, India — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Mumbai  
**Country:** India  
**Themes:** food, culture, nightlife, history

## Attractions

The Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Elephanta Caves, and colonial-era architecture.

## Food

Vada pav, pav bhaji, seafood, Irani cafe fare, and street food at Chowpatty.

## Culture

India's film capital (Bollywood), a fast-paced financial hub with cosmopolitan energy.

## Transportation

Local trains are the city's lifeline; app cabs and autos fill the gaps.

## Safety

One of India's safer big cities; mind crowded trains and traffic.

## Accommodation

Sea-facing luxury hotels in Colaba, mid-range in Bandra, and budget stays near stations.

## Activities

A heritage walk in Fort, an Elephanta ferry, a street-food tour, and a Bollywood studio visit.

## Weather

October is humid post-monsoon; the pleasant, dry season begins in November.

## Local Customs

Dress modestly at religious sites; the city is liberal and welcoming otherwise.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Mumbai: mid-range hotels around $70 per night, daily food budget about $18 per person, and local transport roughly $6 per person per day. Paid attractions average $12 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Mumbai means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
