# Kyoto, Japan — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Kyoto  
**Country:** Japan  
**Themes:** temples, culture, food, gardens, history

## Attractions

Fushimi Inari's torii gates, Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion), Arashiyama bamboo grove, and Gion geisha district.

## Food

Kaiseki multi-course dining, yudofu (tofu hot pot), matcha sweets, and Nishiki Market street food.

## Culture

The best-preserved traditional culture in Japan: geisha, tea ceremony, Zen temples, and kimono rental experiences.

## Transportation

Buses are the main way around; a one-day bus pass is economical, supplemented by two subway lines.

## Safety

Extremely safe; respect photography rules in Gion where harassing geisha is prohibited.

## Accommodation

Traditional ryokan with tatami and onsen, machiya townhouse stays, and modern hotels near Kyoto Station.

## Activities

Temple hopping, a tea ceremony, a kimono walk in Higashiyama, and a bamboo-grove morning stroll.

## Weather

October brings crisp air and early autumn colours; peak foliage arrives in mid-to-late November.

## Local Customs

Reservation-only temples exist; be quiet and respectful in shrines and private gardens.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Kyoto: mid-range hotels around $147 per night, daily food budget about $37 per person, and local transport roughly $13 per person per day. Paid attractions average $26 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Kyoto means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
