# Osaka, Japan — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Osaka  
**Country:** Japan  
**Themes:** food, nightlife, culture, family

## Attractions

Osaka Castle, Dotonbori's neon canal, Universal Studios Japan, and the Umeda Sky Building.

## Food

Osaka is Japan's kitchen: takoyaki, okonomiyaki, kushikatsu, and street food along Dotonbori.

## Culture

Famously friendly, comedic locals; bunraku puppet theatre and a lively down-to-earth atmosphere.

## Transportation

An extensive subway network; the Osaka Amazing Pass bundles transit with attraction entry.

## Safety

Safe and welcoming; the usual care with belongings in crowded nightlife areas.

## Accommodation

Budget-friendly business hotels around Namba and Umeda, plus family options near Universal Studios.

## Activities

A food crawl in Dotonbori, a theme-park day, and a day trip to nearby Nara's deer park.

## Weather

October is comfortable and dry, a good time for walking food tours.

## Local Customs

Stand on the right of escalators (opposite to Tokyo); cash is widely used.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Osaka: mid-range hotels around $147 per night, daily food budget about $37 per person, and local transport roughly $13 per person per day. Paid attractions average $26 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Osaka means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
