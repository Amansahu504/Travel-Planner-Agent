# Paris, France — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Paris  
**Country:** France  
**Themes:** museums, food, romance, art, history

## Attractions

The Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Notre-Dame, Montmartre and Sacre-Coeur, and the Musee d'Orsay.

## Food

Boulangerie croissants, bistro classics, cheese and wine, patisserie, and market picnics along the Seine.

## Culture

World-class art museums, fashion, cafe culture, and river-side book stalls (bouquinistes).

## Transportation

The Metro is dense and cheap; a Navigo or carnet of tickets covers most trips; the RER links the airports.

## Safety

Watch for pickpockets around major sights and on the Metro; keep bags zipped and close.

## Accommodation

Boutique hotels in Le Marais, mid-range options near the Latin Quarter, and luxury near the Champs-Elysees.

## Activities

A Seine river cruise, a day trip to Versailles, and evenings watching the Eiffel Tower sparkle.

## Weather

October is cool (8-16C) with occasional rain; pack layers and an umbrella.

## Local Customs

Greet shopkeepers with 'Bonjour', dining is leisurely, and tipping is modest as service is included.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Paris: mid-range hotels around $147 per night, daily food budget about $37 per person, and local transport roughly $13 per person per day. Paid attractions average $26 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Paris means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
