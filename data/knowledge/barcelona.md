# Barcelona, Spain — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Barcelona  
**Country:** Spain  
**Themes:** art, beach, food, architecture, nightlife

## Attractions

Gaudi's Sagrada Familia and Park Guell, the Gothic Quarter, La Rambla, and city beaches.

## Food

Tapas, paella, jamon, seafood at La Boqueria market, and late-night dining.

## Culture

Catalan modernista architecture, a strong art scene, and vibrant festival calendar.

## Transportation

An easy metro network, plus a walkable old town; the T-casual card offers ten rides.

## Safety

Pickpocketing is common on La Rambla and the metro; stay alert in crowds.

## Accommodation

Design hotels in the Eixample, budget hostels in the Gothic Quarter, and beachside stays.

## Activities

A Gaudi architecture trail, a tapas crawl, a beach afternoon, and a day trip to Montserrat.

## Weather

October is warm and sunny (15-23C), still good for the beach midday.

## Local Customs

Dinner starts late (after 9pm); learn a few Catalan or Spanish greetings.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Barcelona: mid-range hotels around $126 per night, daily food budget about $32 per person, and local transport roughly $11 per person per day. Paid attractions average $22 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Barcelona means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
