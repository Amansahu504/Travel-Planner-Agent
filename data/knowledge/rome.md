# Rome, Italy — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Rome  
**Country:** Italy  
**Themes:** history, food, art, culture

## Attractions

The Colosseum, Roman Forum, Vatican Museums and St. Peter's, the Pantheon, and Trevi Fountain.

## Food

Cacio e pepe, carbonara, Roman-style pizza, supplì, gelato, and espresso at the bar.

## Culture

Millennia of history layered across the city, Baroque squares, and a lively piazza life.

## Transportation

The historic centre is walkable; two metro lines and buses cover longer hops.

## Safety

Beware pickpockets on crowded buses and around major sites; keep bags in front.

## Accommodation

Charming hotels near the Pantheon and Trastevere; budget stays near Termini station.

## Activities

A skip-the-line Colosseum tour, a Vatican morning, and evening strolls through lit piazzas.

## Weather

October is warm and pleasant (12-22C), ideal for walking the ancient sites.

## Local Customs

Dress modestly for churches, cappuccino is a morning drink, and cover-charge (coperto) is normal.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Rome: mid-range hotels around $140 per night, daily food budget about $35 per person, and local transport roughly $12 per person per day. Paid attractions average $25 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Rome means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
