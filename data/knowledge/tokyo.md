# Tokyo, Japan — Destination Guide (Demo)

> **DEMO DATA.** This document is synthetic content written for a demonstration project. Verify all details independently before travelling.

**Destination:** Tokyo  
**Country:** Japan  
**Themes:** food, anime, temples, culture, shopping, technology

## Attractions

Senso-ji temple in Asakusa, the Meiji Shrine, Shibuya Crossing, the teamLab digital art museums, and Tokyo Skytree for skyline views.

## Food

Sushi at Toyosu, ramen in Shinjuku, tempura, monjayaki, izakaya hopping, and conveyor-belt kaiten-zushi for budget dining.

## Culture

Anime and manga culture in Akihabara, sumo tournaments at Ryogoku, traditional tea ceremonies, and karaoke.

## Transportation

The metro and JR Yamanote line cover the city; a Suica/PASMO IC card and a Japan Rail Pass simplify travel.

## Safety

Very low crime; carry cash as many small shops are cash-only; be mindful of typhoon season in September.

## Accommodation

Options range from capsule hotels and business hotels to ryokan and luxury towers in Shinjuku and Ginza.

## Activities

Day trips to Nikko and Kamakura, a Mario-kart style street tour, and seasonal cherry-blossom or autumn-leaf viewing.

## Weather

October is mild and pleasant (15-22C) with autumn foliage; summers are hot and humid.

## Local Customs

Bow when greeting, remove shoes indoors, avoid tipping, keep quiet on trains, and don't eat while walking.

## Budget Planning Notes

Typical demo estimates for Tokyo: mid-range hotels around $147 per night, daily food budget about $37 per person, and local transport roughly $13 per person per day. Paid attractions average $26 per entry. These are planning estimates only, not live prices.

## Suggested Pace

A relaxed pace in Tokyo means two main sights per day plus one meal experience. A packed pace can fit four sights but increases travel fatigue. Group activities by neighbourhood to reduce transit time.
